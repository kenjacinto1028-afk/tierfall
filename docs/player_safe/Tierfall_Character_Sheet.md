# Tierfall — Character Reference Sheet

**Spoiler-free · Starting ages · Character appearance planning**

This sheet contains the **12 major characters** with approved base-portrait directions and **15 secondary characters** whose portraits are planned. The class is inherited **social rank**, while occupation is a person's job or public position. Both matter when designing clothing, tools, uniforms, and facial details. The portraits reflect approved *faces and hairstyles*, not final outfits or equipment.

## The Five Ranks — highest to lowest

| Rank | Metal | Class | Typical work and status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | Platinum | **Crestborn** | Nobles, rulers, political leaders |
| **II** | Steel | **Steelward** | Soldiers, security officers, military leadership |
| **III** | Brass | **Brasswright** | Engineers, physicians, technical specialists |
| **IV** | Copper | **Copperhand** | Clerks, merchants, recordkeepers, logistics staff |
| **V** | Lead | **Leadborn** | Manual workers, service workers, bonded laborers |

Ranks are not jobs: for example, a Brasswright can be a physician or an engineer, while two Crestborn nobles may hold very different offices. Political faction is separate from social rank.

## Protagonist — starting roles

The player begins as **Rank V · Leadborn** and chooses one of four starting occupations:

- **Ward Attendant** — helps care for patients in medical wards
- **Reclamation Worker** — recovers materials and usable technology
- **Farmer** — grows food in controlled colony environments
- **Cargo Runner** — moves freight and supplies through transport hubs

Upbringing is chosen separately: **Family Household**, **Communal Upbringing**, or **Estranged Household**. The protagonist's age is customizable within the agreed **27–30** range.

## Art workflow and boundaries

1. Preserve the approved facial designs, and finish age and unique physical-feature adjustments for **all 12 major characters**.
2. Design visually distinct **clothing, markings, equipment, and gear for each of the Five Ranks**, then personalize by actual job.
3. Design **the six P1 recurring side characters** with a simpler portrait treatment.
4. Design **the remaining nine P2 side characters** with minimal but recognizable portraits.
5. Add later expressions and clothing changes only as needed, without revealing future story details.

**Do not replace existing portraits in the playable game without explicit approval.**
