# Tierfall — Protagonist's Permanent Physical Features

**Player-safe character identity, shared by all starting backgrounds.**

The protagonist always has **exactly two distinctive personal features**, regardless of pronouns, starting occupation, upbringing, or age within the agreed 27–30 range:

1. **Left eyebrow:** A thin diagonal scar interrupting the eyebrow hair.
2. **Right jaw:** A small, pale crescent-shaped healed steam burn immediately beneath the jawline.

## Shared childhood origin

When the protagonist was **twelve**, a pressure door malfunctioned during a power failure in **Thule's living ring**. Its broken latch cut the protagonist's left eyebrow, and escaping steam burned the right side of their jaw. Medical staff treated the injuries, but both marks remained. The event was an ordinary station accident, not a special ability, secret lineage, or predetermined future.

The protagonist also has the legally required **Rank V wrist mark**. That universal social-rank identifier is **not** one of the two personal features.

## Writing and artwork rules

- The features are unconditional for Ward Attendant, Reclamation Worker, Farmer, or Cargo Runner with Family, Communal, or Estranged upbringing.
- Introduce the shared origin in Chapter 1 even when the player skips the prologue. Refer to the features sparingly and naturally later.
- Keep placement, shape, and apparent age of both marks consistent on every future protagonist portrait or facial-expression variant.
- Do not replace current in-game portraits until separately approved. No extra scars or features are required.
