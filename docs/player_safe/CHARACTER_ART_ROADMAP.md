# Tierfall — Spoiler-Free Character Art Roadmap

**Status:** Approved production order; not authorization to update the playable game portraits.  
**Style:** Clean, uncluttered 2D sci-fi graphic novel. Clear ink contours, restrained cel shading, expressive faces, muted colors, simple backgrounds. Preserve each approved face/hair design unless the player requests a revision.

## Phase 1 — Return to all 12 major characters (next)

1. **Age pass:** Adjust the approved designs to match starting ages (below). Use believable lines, hair texture, expression and posture without making every older person look frail or every younger person look alike.
2. **Physical features review:** Confirm facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle, distinctive features, body build, and usual expression. Keep facial identities consistent across future portraits.
3. **Five-Rank clothing and gear system:** Define a shared, easily recognized style for each rank, then give each character a profession-specific look and accessories. Distinguish rank from faction and job.
4. **Character outfit approval:** Review everyday, formal, and job-specific looks where appropriate; keep story-specific outfits hidden and avoid future plot spoilers.
5. **Expression set:** Once the base look is approved, generate a restrained expression set and optional appearance evolution for later chapters.

## Five Ranks: clothing language (to finalize together)

| Rank | Distinctive visual language | Gear examples to consider |
|---|---|---|
| V · **Leadborn** | Durable reused fabrics, repair seams, practical layers | Work straps, utility pouches, protective gear |
| IV · **Copperhand** | Neat fitted workwear, small copper trim, document-friendly pockets | Ledger slate, seals, trade or record cases |
| III · **Brasswright** | Precise technical uniforms, brass identifiers, specialist details | Diagnostic tools, engineer or medical equipment |
| II · **Steelward** | Structured protective clothing, formal service marks, practical armor | Tactical communications, duty gear, field packs |
| I · **Crestborn** | Fine tailoring, restrained platinum details, ceremonial silhouettes | Lineage seal, status tokens, official accessories |

**Rule:** Do not treat rank as a faction or assume every person of a rank dresses identically. Clothes must also suit age, profession, location, and personality. Do not introduce story-revealing costumes in player-facing reviews.

## Asset production / quality checks

- Age-correct portraits for all 12 leads; track approved facial features and palette so later expressions maintain identity.
- Distinguish skin tone, hairstyle, silhouette, facial features and approximate age across the cast; avoid reused template faces.
- Show draft previews in **batches of three major characters**, awaiting the player's selection/feedback before advancing.
- **Do not overwrite playable HTML art, portrait slots or saves without the player's explicit approval.**
- Keep detailed story-specific appearance triggers out of player-safe documents.
