# TierFall — Act I Audit Revision Plan (player-safe)

**Approved decisions:** 2026-11-21. **Status:** Chapter 1 updated publicly; Chapters 2–5 revised privately and awaiting independent editorial review, human-timed acceptance and live end-to-end QA.

## Revision order

1. **Opening continuity:** correct three wording/choice discrepancies in the released Chapter 1, without changing its decision outcomes.
2. **Chronology and prose:** repair scene-order and day/time discontinuities; remove editor/QA instructions accidentally shown to players; eliminate repeated introductions and repetitive dialogue.
3. **Character and daily-life continuity:** make a confirmed supporting character occupation consistent, reflect ongoing work responsibilities, and improve origin- and relationship-specific callbacks.
4. **Story depth:** expand the shorter concluding Act I chapters with dramatized reactions, consequences for visibility and responsibility, and an earned chapter-to-act transition. Preserve unreleased mysteries.
5. **Choices and state:** audit proof provenance and conditional scenes, carry early decisions through the Act I finale, and keep protected information tied to consent.
6. **No-save game mode:** remove local game-progress persistence, save/import/export controls, and saved-game return prompts. Playthrough starts fresh on reload; within-session choices and journal remain.
7. **Acceptance QA:** validate canon, character consistency, chronology, all 12 starting backgrounds, major choice branches, speaker and naming tokens, scene transitions and browser/mobile presentation; target approximately 2–2.5 hours of representative Act I reading, prioritizing pacing.

**Publishing restriction:** this repository is PUBLIC. This plan intentionally contains no unreleased scenes, event details, plot secrets, or confidential dialogue. Protected chapter manuscripts and audit details remain private.

**Non-goals:** portrait replacement, release publication, and GitHub Pages configuration changes.
