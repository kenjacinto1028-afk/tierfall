## Portrait treatment

- **P1 — Six core secondary characters:** clean graphic-novel bust portraits with strong facial identity, age-appropriate features, class-appropriate clothing, and approximately **2 expressions** initially. Less detail than the 12 major characters.
- **P2 — Nine lighter secondary characters:** one clear bust portrait per person; add a second expression only if their scenes make it worthwhile.
- **One-scene extras:** silhouettes, simple portraits, or no portrait, depending on their role.
- All portraits use the approved **clean, uncluttered comic/visual-novel style**. Different faces, hair types, skin tones, jobs, and ages are important. **Class clothing and gear will be designed after the major cast's age and physical-feature review.**

## Writing and continuity rules

- A returning character must be recognizable by name, rank, and appearance, and should remember relevant player choices.
- Do not force every side character into every route. Reappearances should feel natural and serve a scene.
- A side character can be more or less prominent depending on the player's background and choices, but their core public identity remains consistent.
- **Lady Coris Vale** and major character **Garric Vale** share a surname. This does **not** establish a family connection; any connection must be deliberately authored and approved under spoiler-safe development rules.
- No character here has a fixed betrayal, death, romantic path, or story outcome in this player-safe roster.

**Counts:** 15 side characters = 3 per rank; 6 P1 core, 6 reappearing P2, 3 short-arc P2. This is a planning list, not a promise that each character has appeared in the current playable Chapter 1.
