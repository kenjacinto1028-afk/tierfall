## Writing and audit contract
Use readable **second-person present-tense English**, human dialogue with distinct voices, ordinary-life warmth and credible humor alongside high stakes; avoid repetitive cliffhangers and exposition dumps. Aim for meaningful decisions with immediate and later route-aware reactions. Choices can reconverge but must not be erased. Romance is optional, adult, reciprocal and never required for essential plot information; pronouns must not exclude approved eligibility.

Check chronological and geographic continuity, age/marks, rank-access constraints, testimony/record/evidence provenance, who knows what on each route, old save migration, glossary/journal/recap reveal gating, witness consent, and scene-to-scene bridges. For optional old save flags, missing is not automatically the same as `false`.
