# Tierfall — Spoiler-Free Player Brief

**Safe to read. No plot twists, secret character motives, hidden branches, or ending requirements are included here.**

## The game you approved
Tierfall is a choice-driven science-fiction RPG told through a visual-novel-style browser interface. A playthrough targets **6–8 hours** across **three acts**. Writing uses clear, natural English for non-native English readers. The game focuses on relationships, difficult decisions, and meaningful consequences rather than detailed management or grid combat.

- **Starting job:** choose Ward Attendant, Reclamation Worker, Farmer, or Cargo Runner; random selection is also possible.
- **Upbringing:** choose Family Household, Communal Upbringing, or Estranged Household.
- **Society:** five clearly numbered social ranks; social rank is not the same as faction or job.
- **Politics:** three large factions with internal disagreements. The player can shift allegiances.
- **Characters:** aim for at least twelve recurring major characters, with optional romance.
- **Consequences:** choices affect people and future opportunities; failed choices usually lead to more story, not an immediate restart.
- **Presentation:** readable text, dialogue, portraits, occasional background art, prominent choices, journal, desktop/mobile support, and in-session progress without a save system.

## What you can approve without spoilers
UI layout, art direction, reading comfort, length, pacing, choices per scene, difficulty, save options, romance boundaries, and how much past-choice information the journal reveals.

## Safe development updates
Report numbers and quality results only: chapters drafted, scenes and meaningful choices added, background variations tested, broken routes, save/load reliability, readability, and estimated playtime. Do **not** list exact events, secrets, betrayals, twist outcomes, or unlock requirements.
